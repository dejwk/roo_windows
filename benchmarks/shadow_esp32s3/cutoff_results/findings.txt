This option is exact for black shadows blended over already-quantized RGB565
pixels using roo_display decoding/blending/encoding; composition over RGB888
colors can differ. It is opt-in and disabled by default. Unit tests cover all
65536 RGB565 destinations and all 32 elevations, four zooms, three cache modes.

Interpretation and limits:
Identical-coordinate sampling is +0.23% across scenes, consistent with no
material shader overhead. Native and tiled sampling save 11.13% and 12.30%
in total, respectively; their normalized costs change +0.74% and -0.58%.
The full-bounds denominator changes the mix of interior/exterior pixels.
Single-widget refresh results are mixed: -4.04% to +8.36%, +0.54% geometric
mean. The no-shadow control also regresses 5.45%, so these refresh differences
cannot all be attributed to the shadow bounds. This run does not establish a
general refresh speedup. Twelve level-3 widgets improve 3.34%.
