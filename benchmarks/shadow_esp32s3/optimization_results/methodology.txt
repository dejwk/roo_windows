Optimization and precomputed-gradient benchmark methodology

Makerfabs MaTouch ESP32-S3 Parallel TFT 5 inch; CPU 240 MHz, flash 80 MHz,
8 MB OPI PSRAM, 800x480 RGB565 panel at 16 MHz PCLK. Panel DMA remains active.
All global rendering definitions agree across roo_windows translation units.
Two complete USB-triggered runs per variant, seven timed batches per run.
Reported values are the median of all 14 batch means, with ranges and standard
deviation retained in summary.csv. Serial output, setup and gallery rendering
are outside timed sections.

Metrics:
fixed512: 150 iterations per batch after 10 warmups; exactly the same 512 exterior
  sample coordinates in each renderer. Divide by 512 for ns/pixel and by CPU
  period for cycles/pixel. This measures decoration sampling, not framebuffer I/O.
native: 12 iterations per batch after 3 warmups; readColors scanlines across the
  entire decoration extent, including opaque interior and transparent samples.
  Divide by the recorded pixels field (actual extent area).
tiles8: same iteration count, but readColorRect in 8x8 tiles across the extent.
  Its normalized cost includes the benefit of constant-span and uniform-block
  reuse. It is not the cost of evaluating each shadow sample independently.
refresh1/refresh12: 25 application refreshes per batch after an initial frame
  and 25 warmup refreshes. Invalidate widget interiors, then run normal painting,
  composition and RGB565 framebuffer output. Report per refresh; the pixels
  field only counts logical widget area and is not all framebuffer/shadow work.

The harness adds 0/4/8/12/20/24px radii to the previous scenes; the 56px level-3
surface supplies radius16. Radius28 and mixed4/12/18/8 exercise cache fallback.
All six cached radii are timed at level3; radius16 additionally at levels0/1/5,
and a 240x160 radius12 menu at level2. Host tests cover every level at every
supported zoom (75/100/150/200). Hardware timings use zoom100 only.

Core optimizations retain every 8-bit output alpha: prune only already-zero LUT
tails, reuse constant row/column spans, share nearest-edge geometry between both
layers, and use an exact integer Newton square root. Bounds shrink from 86x86 to 80x80 for the level3 56px example: 13.47% fewer
logical output pixels. No support reduction is attributed to gradient caching;
all three optimized modes use the same bounds and shared geometry setup.

Side profiles combine both layers, offsets and source-over in advance. One
horizontal profile serves both left and right; top and bottom have separate
profiles. Five nonzero levels times three profiles times 65 samples = 975 bytes
at zoom100. Corner patches store combined alpha for six radii, five levels and
two vertical orientations; left/right are mirrored. There are 46,402 pixel bytes
and 480 descriptor bytes, in addition to the 975 side bytes. Only the selected
zoom is compiled. Unsupported radii and mixed quadrant selections reuse the
resolved geometry and compute exact alpha. No pre-blending with a background
or RGB565 quantization is baked into the cache.

These are warmed repeated-scene measurements. Cold-cache latency and workloads
alternating many levels/radii while streaming unrelated images are not measured.
Binary layout can affect ESP32 timing, including the flat level0 control; small
cross-firmware differences should not be interpreted as universal guarantees.
The fresh double_flash control uses this expanded harness. Its values need not
match the older eight-scene historical binary in ../results/ exactly.

All four binaries reserve 30,776 static RAM bytes and each Decoration remains
84 bytes. The older harness used 30,632 bytes; the 144-byte difference is the six
additional benchmark scenes, not renderer state. LUTs and gradients are verified
at DROM addresses in the linked ELF. Firmware size, data symbols and hashes are
in manifest.json; data-only costs at every zoom are in gradient-storage.json.

The initial cache dispatcher is retained in dispatch_initial/. It repeated
geometry work on a miss. Final results use a dispatcher that reuses the nearest-
edge coordinates for fallback. The final no-gradient baseline
uses the same shared geometry setup as the cache modes. Control and side-cache
runs preceded that baseline-only code change: their final rebuilt executable
and rendering-data sections are byte-identical, as recorded in
shared-base-other-variants-equivalence.json. Only build metadata/debug
information may differ. The baseline and corner-cache runs use final binaries.
